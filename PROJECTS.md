# Selected Program & Operations Work

Case studies from program and portfolio management roles across telecom, manufacturing, defense, and finance-sector organizations. Company names are omitted by preference; all figures and outcomes below are accurate to the underlying work.

---

## Building a KPI/OKR Framework from Scratch for a $25M+ IT & Finance Portfolio

**Context:** Stepped into a portfolio-health and executive-liaison role for a combined IT and Finance portfolio with no existing performance-tracking structure in place.

**What was done:**
- Designed and built a KPI/OKR tracking framework from the ground up, covering delivery health, risk exposure, and resource allocation across the portfolio.
- Moved a cross-regional team of 20+ from a traditional delivery model to Agile.
- Renegotiated key vendor contracts to reduce cost and shorten delivery cycles.

**Results:**
- 35% efficiency gain within 12 months of the framework going live.
- Escalations to executive leadership cut in half.
- Time-to-delivery down 30%; roughly 20% in hard costs removed through automation and better headcount alignment.

## Standing Up a Formal PMO for a $68M Cross-Border Portfolio

**Context:** A $68M project portfolio spanning North America and Latin America, with 50+ employees, contractors, and vendor partners working across parallel workstreams, had no centralized program governance.

**What was done:**
- Created the organization's first formal Project Management Office (PMO): wrote the governance framework, standardized status/risk reporting across all workstreams, and introduced consistent intake and prioritization processes.
- Overhauled vendor contracts and supply-chain workflows.
- Built an M&A integration playbook that was later used to guide two subsequent acquisitions.

**Results:**
- Logistics and shipping costs down 25%.
- Total annual operating expenses down 15%.
- Repeatable integration playbook adopted for future M&A activity.

## Operational Audit and IT Security Program in a Regulated Environment

**Context:** A business unit operating in a compliance-sensitive (defense-adjacent) environment needed to reduce cost of goods sold and close out a security infrastructure upgrade without disrupting client delivery.

**What was done:**
- Ran a full operational audit with key clients, tightening shipping and order fulfillment end to end.
- Delivered a complex IT security upgrade program, managing scope, schedule, and budget simultaneously.
- Conducted market and competitive analysis to surface expansion opportunities that had not previously been quantified.

**Results:**
- COGS reduced by 23%.
- Security program delivered at a 92% on-scope / on-budget / on-time completion rate; reduced regulatory risk exposure.
- Findings from the competitive analysis fed directly into the organization's annual revenue growth plan.

## Cutting Audit Findings and Compliance Violations by 75%

**Context:** A multi-program portfolio needed better data quality and financial visibility after several years of recurring audit findings.

**What was done:**
- Deployed new operational technology and internal tracking systems to close visibility gaps in financial and compliance reporting.
- Owned forecasting cycles, P&L analysis, and ROI modeling behind the organization's largest investment decisions.
- Held the multi-program portfolio on time and on budget through two years of shifting priorities, scope changes, and leadership turnover.

**Results:**
- Financial audit findings and compliance violations down 75%.
- Materially reduced portfolio risk exposure heading into subsequent budget cycles.

### Change Management Issues in a Large Multinational Corporation: A Study of People and Systems

**Authors:** Adeel Ali, Sergey Ivanov, Ph.D.
**Journal:** International Journal of Organizational Innovation (ISSN 1943-1813)
**Volume:** 8, Number 1
**Date:** July 2015
**Pages:** 24–30
**Publisher:** International Association of Organizational Innovation (IAOI)
**Link:** https://www.ijoi-online.org/attachments/article/45/FINAL%20ISSUE%20VOL%208%20NUM%201%20JULY%202015.pdf#page=24

**Summary:** The paper examines a large North American telecommunications company
(wireless, residential, and enterprise service lines) that was experiencing repeated
leadership turnover and its downstream effects on employee morale, cross-team
communication, and strategic direction. Using W. Edwards Deming's fourteen points of
management and Elliott Jaques' General Theory of Managerial Hierarchy (Stratified
Systems Theory), the authors trace organizational dysfunction to misalignment between
the formal hierarchy and the actual scope/timespan of work at each level, and offer
management recommendations — clearer strategic purpose, breaking down departmental
silos, reducing fear-driven management practices, and investing in employee training
and development — to improve organizational effectiveness.

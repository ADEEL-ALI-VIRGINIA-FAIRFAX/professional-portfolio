## Publications

- Ali, A., & Ivanov, S. (2015). *Change Management Issues in a Large Multinational
  Corporation: A Study of People and Systems.* **International Journal of
  Organizational Innovation**, 8(1), 24–30.
  [Full issue (PDF), p. 24](https://www.ijoi-online.org/attachments/article/45/FINAL%20ISSUE%20VOL%208%20NUM%201%20JULY%202015.pdf#page=24)

  A peer-reviewed case study applying Deming's management principles and Elliott
  Jaques' General Theory of Managerial Hierarchy to analyze change-management
  challenges — communication breakdowns across teams, low morale, resistance to
  change, and lack of strategic direction — at a large North American
  telecommunications company, with recommendations for leadership and process
  improvement.
